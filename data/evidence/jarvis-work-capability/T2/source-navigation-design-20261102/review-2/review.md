**B · P2 QUALITY · design.md:66 and revision-2/analysis.json proposed_new_budget_only.maximum_overflow_sentinel_bytes.** The public HTTPX response iterator can bound bytes admitted to validation/storage. It cannot guarantee that the client has actually downloaded only one extra byte. In the installed httpx/_models.py:935–956, iter_raw marks its stream consumed, constructs a fixed-size ByteChunker and increments num_bytes_downloaded by the length of the entire underlying stream block before yielding its first chunk. A second iterator cannot change the chunk size midway. Even chunk_size=1 on the first iterator does not prevent read-ahead. httpx/_decoders.py:233–256 buffers/splits the incoming block; httpcore/_sync/http11.py:44,217–218 requests up to 64 KiB. If a 4 KiB body block arrives with 512 bytes of allowance left, underlying body-byte growth can be 4 KiB while only 512 bytes plus one sentinel are inspected. A receipt claiming actual bytes equal cap+1 would understate a known client-side observation. Specify hard caps on retained/admitted bytes, keep a sentinel count for consumer observation, record actual HTTPX body bytes via num_bytes_downloaded including overshoot on early close/error, and identify unavailable counts below that layer as unknown. No custom transport or socket-level guarantee is needed. Require a future test with a single deliberately oversized underlying block near the boundary, status/error and early close paths and independent assertions for all counters and zero promotion. Do not change POST limits, deadlines or the one-attempt policy.
