# Independent semantic rubric (freeze before model use)

Give the reviewer only the four shuffled P packets and this rubric. Score each packet before opening the assignment key. Include substantive titles, section headings and extra claims. Preserve the writer's project attribution, polarity, scope and conditions. For each distinct assertion record its wording, source identity, relevance and these two independent judgments:

1. **Truth in the intended full archived source.** Record supported, contradicted, or insufficient, with a short quotation and line numbers from that full source. If the source identity cannot be determined from the proposal's own attribution and the question, record attribution unknown and truth insufficient. For cross-source claims identify all claimed parties and look for evidence of the actual relationship. Do not search other files for substitute sources.
2. **Support by the submitted citation.** Independently record supported, contradicted, insufficient, missing, or invalid. Use only the citation that the proposal submitted, including the span resolved directly from its submitted, source-scoped reference. A located range may be unrelated. Check neighboring context for reversals of meaning. Missing and invalid remain distinct. Do not repair a miscopied line, supply a handle, or transfer a reviewer-discovered range from the full-source column. For substantive headings or titles, assess support from the scope's actually submitted claims/citations together. Unsupported framing counts even if all underlying facts are true. Pure labels have no substantive assertion.

Record branch polarity and project attribution separately. A true claim whose submitted excerpt says something else remains a citation failure; a syntactically valid local call cannot establish every assertion about runtime or cross-project integration. Record per-column counts and per-case coverage. Do not merge true-but-miscited claims into the same tally as claims contradicted by the full source.

**Noncanonical text.** Packets preserve proposed titles and the ordered source/heading/claims, the proposal state, and a common fallback_text field. For an invalid action/report the field contains the original secured visible content, including readable malformed prose, final_answer text and nonstandard or bare-string facts. For a mechanically valid proposal it can hold optional thought text. With no visible text record that explicitly. Report any recoverable substantive assertions from fallback separately, with the same truth and citation judgments. Count a verbatim or equivalent repetition of an already assessed assertion once and mark overlap. Noncanonical text may expose field style and compromise blinding; record that possibility without looking up the assignment. Never infer a report, grant coverage or credit a missing citation from a fallback.

**Coverage.** E02 needs at least three distinct relevant sources, an evidenced desktop behavior or architecture fact, active agent/provider wiring or task execution, and a genuine observation about the open-source base or a properly scoped distinction supported by available sources. Check each provider claim against the branch. The held-out case requires supported material from all three files on secure write, sensitive-path handling and signature verification, including an accurately distinguished error or fallback branch.

**Joint decision.** Missing/malformed reports cannot pass. Semantic pass requires complete coverage and correct attribution; all substantive report assertions must be supported in their intended source(s) and by their actual applicable submitted citations, and relevant. A contradictory or unsupported substantive fallback assertion must also be reported and prevents promotion. Grade fallback separately and never use it to turn a failed report into a passing one. Mechanical pass independently requires the exact report action/envelope, scoped path and unchanged renderer. Only a handle variant passing both gates on both cases, improving joint outcome on E02 and preserving the held-out outcome, merits considering an interface implementation. Both variants passing both cases is non-discriminating. Stop if mechanical gains leave incorrect grounding. No diagnostic outcome constitutes E02 acceptance.
